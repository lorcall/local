Privacy Policy

LoCal · Last updated 12 November 2026

LoCal keeps your food log in your own Google Drive. There is no LoCal server holding your data, no account to create, and no database of users' meals anywhere.

What LoCal stores, and where

When you first sign in, LoCal creates a folder called LoCal App in your Google Drive containing:

  • LoCal log — a Google Sheet holding your meals, food items, saved favourites, targets and weigh-ins.
  • LoCal photos — a folder holding the meal photos you take.

These files belong to you. You can open, edit, export or delete them at any time without using LoCal, and your Google storage quota is what holds them.

What LoCal can see in your Drive

LoCal requests a single Google permission, drive.file. This grants access only to the files LoCal itself created for you. LoCal cannot see, list or open anything else in your Drive — not your other documents, photos or folders — even if it wanted to.

Meal analysis and Google Gemini

When you ask LoCal to estimate a meal, the photo or description you provide is sent to a small service run by LoCal's developer, which passes it to Google's Gemini API and returns the estimate. That service does not store your photos or descriptions; it holds them only for the moment it takes to answer.

Gemini is used on Google's free tier. Under Google's free-tier terms, Google may use content submitted through that API to improve their products. If you would rather a meal not be sent to Google, log it with Quick add, a saved favourite, or a copied meal — none of those call Gemini.

Weekly summaries send only computed numbers, never photos.

Your email address

LoCal is shared with a small number of people, so the analysis service keeps an approval list. When you first sign in, your email address is recorded there with your approval status, and the developer is emailed so they can approve you. You may receive an email when you are approved.

Your email address is used for nothing else. It is not shared, sold, or used for marketing.

What is kept on your device

LoCal stores a few things in your browser so it can find your files and work smoothly: the IDs of your LoCal Sheet and photo folder, and a count of how many analyses you have run today. Clearing your browser data removes these; your log in Drive is unaffected.

LoCal sets no advertising or tracking cookies and uses no analytics. Nobody is counting your visits.

Deleting your data

Because your data lives in your Drive, you remove it the same way you remove any file:

  • Delete the LoCal App folder from your Google Drive. That is all of it.
  • Revoke LoCal's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • To have your email removed from the approval list, contact the developer below.

Children

LoCal is not intended for children under 13, and is not directed at them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. LoCal is a personal project shared with friends, so changes will be small and infrequent.
